Project Overview

Corellian has been a key partner in delivering critical rigging and installation services for Sydney's expanding metro rail network. Our work encompasses major infrastructure projects including complex installations at Sydney Central Station and various metro sites throughout the network.

Our comprehensive services for these metro projects include precision rigging of heavy equipment, TVS and electrical equipment, switchboard installations, and damper and attenuator installations. This is coordinated with multiple stakeholders to ensure seamless project delivery in active rail environments while maintaining strict safety protocols.
